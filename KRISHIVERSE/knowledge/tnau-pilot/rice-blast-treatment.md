**Rice blast treatment (TNAU)** — dosage-bearing record.

Chemical: Tricyclazole 75% WP @1g/liter water or Carbendazim 50% WP @1g/liter water; 3 to 4 sprays at nursery, tillering, booting (panicle initiation) and flowering stages.

Organic: Pseudomonas fluorescens talc formulation — dry seed treatment @10g/kg of seed; foliar spray @0.5% from 45 days after transplanting at 10 day intervals, three times.

Immediate: Remove collateral weed hosts from bunds and channels; burn straw and stubbles after harvest.

Source: TNAU (Tamil Nadu Agricultural University).
