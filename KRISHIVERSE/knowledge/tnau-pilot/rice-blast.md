**Rice Blast (Blast Disease)** — TNAU agronomic knowledge.

Symptoms: diamond-shaped lesions with gray centers and brown margins on leaves; whitish-gray spots with brown edges; neck blast blackening the neck and dropping the panicle; node blast blackening nodes.

Crop: Rice. Favorable conditions: high humidity, prolonged leaf wetness, heavy nitrogen.

Prevention: use resistant varieties (IR-64, Jaya); balanced fertilization — avoid excess nitrogen; proper water management; seed treatment; maintain field hygiene.
